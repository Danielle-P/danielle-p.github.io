UX Diploma Coursework

As part of my Professional Diploma in UX Design (UX Design Institute), I worked through a structured, end-to-end process to design a flight booking experience for a fictional airline, "Fly UX".

The project focused on applying user-centred design methodology rigorously: conducting usability research and creating empathy maps to designing detailed interaction flows based on data.

Research & Analysis

I conducted a series of research activities to inform my design process, including:

Competitive benchmarking

Analysed flight booking flows from multiple airlines to understand common patterns

Identified best practices and opportunities for improvement

Usability testing

Led moderated usability tests with 2 users booking flights on competitor sites

Observed where they struggled, what they expected, and where friction occurred

Affinity mapping

Grouped research findings into themes and patterns using affinity diagrams in Miro

Identified the most critical usability issues and organised them by journey stage

Empathy Map

Collected qualitative data from user interviews and usability tests

Created an empathy map showing user goals, behaviours, context, and pain points across the journey to visualise what users were thinking and feeling at critical moments.

The research revealed several recurring problems: users found fare comparison tables confusing, baggage selection processes were unclear, and there was too much cognitive load at checkout. These insights became the foundation for my design decisions.
